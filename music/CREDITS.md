# Music credits

`christmas-lofi.mp3` is a ~36-minute mix of the 11 tracks below, in this order. Every
track is either dedicated to the public domain (CC0) or licensed for reuse with credit
(CC BY 4.0), as stated on each track's Free Music Archive page.

**Changes made:** each track was loudness-normalized, joined with 2-second crossfades, and
re-encoded to a single 192 kbps MP3 (`tools/mix-music.sh`).
